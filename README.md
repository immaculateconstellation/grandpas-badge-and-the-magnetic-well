# Grandpa's Badge and the Magnetic Well

**A story about the light that holds us**
**By Gregory Scott Davis**

A children's picture book written from the author's faith as a member of
The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. When Gregory was a little
boy, his grandpa made him a badge — a copper five-pointed star set in a
stainless steel ring — and the story follows the badge into the magnetic
well, where the light holds it.
